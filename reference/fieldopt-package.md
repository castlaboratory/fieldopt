# fieldopt: Field-Survey Design under Real Field Cost

Design of area and list sample surveys that go to the field, minimising
the real cost of field work (travel between sampling units plus
interviews) subject to a target variance of the design-based estimator.
Travel matrices from coordinates or a road network, GRASP routing of the
field work for one or several teams, spatially balanced selection of
units with the local pivotal method, Horvitz-Thompson estimation with
the local-mean variance estimator, cost-aware allocation across strata
or frames, and the cost-variance frontier of a design by simulation. The
computational engine is the Rust crate fieldopt-core.
