# fieldopt: Area-Frame and Dual-Frame Survey Design under Real Field Cost

Design of agricultural and other area-frame and dual-frame (area plus
list) sample surveys that go to the field, with the real cost of field
work (travel between sampling units plus interviews) next to the
variance of the design-based estimator. Travel matrices from coordinates
or a road network; GRASP routing of the field work for one or several
teams; stratified spatially balanced (local pivotal) or spatially
ordered systematic selection of segments with interpenetrating
replicates; points inside segments with multiplicity (expected-hits)
estimation; closed, open and weighted segment estimators;
Horvitz-Thompson estimation with local-mean or replicate variances;
cost-aware allocation across strata, Hartley's dual-frame allocation
with an overlap, and two-stage allocation with a cost function; and the
cost-variance frontier of a design by simulation. The computational
engine is the Rust crate 'fieldopt-core'.
